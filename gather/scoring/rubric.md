# Idea scoring rubric

Score each idea 0-100 as the sum of the criteria below, minus the overpromise penalty. Be strict: a 60 is a good idea, an 80 is one you would post today.

| Criterion | Max | Full marks when |
|---|---|---|
| hold | 20 | A reader would stop scrolling and read to the end: a concrete situation, a real stake, a reveal that pays off. |
| send | 15 | Someone would forward it to a specific colleague ("you need to see this"). |
| proof | 20 | It rests on something real: a number from your own work, a screenshot, a before/after, a named public example. Zero if the proof would have to be invented. |
| hook | 15 | The first line works alone and makes a specific promise. |
| topic | 15 | It sits in the topics your audience follows you for (see VOICE.md / TOPICS.md) and resembles a past winner. |
| real_work | 10 | It comes from something you actually did or shipped recently, not a generic opinion. |
| tools | 5 | It names the actual tools or steps, so it's copyable. |

**Overpromise penalty (0 to -15):** subtract when the hook claims more than the body can deliver, or when it implies results the source material does not show.

For each idea also return the closest past winner (a URL from winners.csv, or empty) and one sentence on why it scored as it did.

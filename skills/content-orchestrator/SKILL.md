# Content orchestrator

Move one piece of content through these steps. Do one step, show the result, wait for a go-ahead before the next.

1. **Intake.** Where did the idea come from? A brief item (`gather/data/brief-*.md`), a note, a call, something shipped. Record the source.
2. **Angle.** One claim, who it's for, which channel. If you can't say it in one sentence, stop and ask.
3. **Draft.** Read the author's VOICE.md and BOUNDARIES.md first, every time. Draft in their voice. Use only facts and numbers from the source.
4. **Slop pass.** Run the `no-ai-slop` skill. Keep the writer's voice; cut the AI patterns.
5. **Pack.** Only the variants needed: X post, LinkedIn version (`repurpose/prompts/x-to-linkedin.md`), graphic (`repurpose/graphics`), quote post.
6. **Review.** Show the author the exact final text of every variant, plus where links go (first comment) and when it would post.
7. **Schedule.** Only after an explicit yes on that exact text: create drafts in Typefully (`repurpose/typefully.md`). List the drafts afterwards and confirm what will go out and when.

Rules:
- Never post, schedule, reply or send anything without the author's explicit yes on the final text.
- Never invent numbers, names, quotes or results. Missing proof means a weaker claim, not a made-up one.
- Private stays private: clients, money, internal tools and spend, people who haven't posted publicly.
- No LinkedIn automation beyond creating Typefully drafts the author approves.
- At most one visual post per day on X; links in the first comment; a "link in the comments" line only when the comment really carries a link, never filler; no "here's how" lines; quote tweets use a GIF or video, never stills; graphics ship in dark and light mode.
